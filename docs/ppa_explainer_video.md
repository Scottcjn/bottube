# What is Proof of Physical AI? – Explainer Video

**Duration:** 1‑3 minutes  
**Format:** Screen‑recorded slides with voice‑over (MP4)  

## Overview
This short explainer covers the core concepts of **Proof of Physical AI (PPA)** and why it matters for decentralized physical infrastructure networks (DePIN). The video walks through:

1. **What is PPA?** – The missing layer that ties AI computation to verifiable hardware.
2. **Hardware fingerprinting** – A simple explanation of the 7 channels used to uniquely identify a device.
3. **Vintage advantage** – Why a 2003 PowerPC G4 can earn more than a modern Threadripper.
4. **Every machine becomes vintage** – The insight that hardware value appreciates as it ages.
5. **GPU spoofing detection** – How we caught 9 fake GPUs using fingerprint anomalies.
